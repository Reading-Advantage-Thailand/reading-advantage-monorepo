# Tower Defense.zip - Asset Descriptions

This archive contains two tower defense tileset packs. The assets are static environment tiles and flags, not character sprites.

## Tower Defense Pack 01

- `tower_defense_01.png`: blue-gray castle tileset with walls, towers, gates, stone blocks, posts, barrels, and rubble.
- `tower_defense_01_a.png`: cream and tan palette of the Pack 01 castle tileset.
- `tower_defense_01_b.png`: pale blue palette of the Pack 01 castle tileset.
- `tower_defense_01_c.png`: dark charcoal palette of the Pack 01 castle tileset.
- `tower_defense_01_d.png`: additional dark palette of the Pack 01 castle tileset.
- `tower_defense_flags.png`: shield-shaped flags in blue, red, purple, and gold.
- `tower_defense_flags_a.png`: blue flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_b.png`: red and orange flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_c.png`: purple and pink flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_d.png`: gold and yellow flag palette.

## Tower Defense Pack 02

- `tower_defense_02.png`: larger blue-gray fortress tileset with towers, battlements, gates, walls, posts, and rubble.
- `tower_defense_02_a.png`: cream and tan palette of the Pack 02 fortress tileset.
- `tower_defense_02_b.png`: pale blue palette of the Pack 02 fortress tileset.
- `tower_defense_02_c.png`: dark charcoal palette of the Pack 02 fortress tileset.
- `tower_defense_02_d.png`: additional dark palette of the Pack 02 fortress tileset.
- `tower_defense_flags.png`: shield-shaped flags in blue, red, purple, and gold.
- `tower_defense_flags_a.png`: blue flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_b.png`: red and orange flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_c.png`: purple and pink flag palette.
- `tower_defense_flags_d.png`: gold and yellow flag palette.
