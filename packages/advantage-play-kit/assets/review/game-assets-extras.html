# Game Assets [Extras].zip - Asset Descriptions

This archive is a bundle of seven smaller free asset packs covering characters, dungeon tilesets, minerals, treasure chests, dominoes, playing cards, and a farming-game cellar dungeon. Each sub-zip can be used independently.

## Domino Pixelart Asset Pack

Pixel-art dominoes with full black-and-white sets.

- Domino_Pieces_Down.png, Domino_Pieces_Left.png, Domino_Pieces_Right.png, Domino_Pieces_Up.png: orientation sprites for white-pip dominoes.
- Domino_Pieces_Down_Black.png, _Left_Black.png, _Right_Black.png, _Up_Black.png: orientation sprites for black-pip dominoes.
- Domino Down/, Domino Left/, Domino Right/, Domino Up/: individual domino sprites per orientation.
- Domino Black Down/, Black Left/, Black Right/, Black Up/: black-pip variants per orientation.

## Farm Game Dungeon Cellar Tileset

Tileset and characters for the farming-game cellar dungeon.

- Tilesets/FG_Cellar.png: full cellar tileset.
- Tilesets/FG_Cellar_Doors.png: cellar door variants.
- Characters/ folder: humanoid knight characters (Knight_10 through Knight_20) with idle, axe, hoe, pickaxe, watering can, sword, and dead animation frames in four directions.
- RPG Maker/ folder: RPG Maker builds.

## Free Character Sprites - Fantasy Dreamland

Six humanoid Fantasy Dreamland characters.

- Char_001 through Char_006: each folder provides full sprite sheets plus an Idle sheet.
- 16x16/, 32x32/, 48x48/ folders: character sprites in three sizes.
- RPG Maker/ folder: MV, MZ (16/32/48), and VX Ace builds.

## Free Dungeon - Fantasy Dreamland

- 16x16/, 32x32/, 48x48/ folders: dungeon tile sprites.
- FD_Dungeon_Free.png: full dungeon sheet.
- RPG Maker/ folder: prebuilt MV, MZ, and VX Ace builds.
- License.txt: usage terms.

## Free Fantasy Dreamland Minerals

- 16x16/, 32x32/, 48x48/ folders: mineral and ore sprites.
- FD_Minerals_Stones.png: full minerals sheet.
- License.txt: usage terms.

## Free Treasure Chests

- 16x16/, 32x32/ folders: treasure-chest sprites.
- RGW_Chests.png: full chest sprite sheet.
- RPG Maker MV-MZ/ folder: prebuilt RPG Maker chest assets.
- License.txt: usage terms.

## Playing Cards Pixelart Asset Pack

Full 52-card deck plus extras in pixel art.

- Playing_Cards.png: full deck sprite sheet.
- Sprites/ folder: Card Back 1.png through Card Back 3.png and individual Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts, Spades from 1 through King.
- License.txt: usage terms.
- Read Me.txt: usage instructions.

## Notes

This archive contains environment tiles, props, characters, and game pieces only. It does not include monsters, creatures, or undead sprites.
