# Platformer World.zip - Asset Descriptions

This archive contains a complete pixel-art 2D platformer kit with heroes, enemies, tilesets, animated objects, backgrounds, items, and sound effects. Engine-ready builds are included for GameMaker, Godot, and Unity. The archive contains 28 numbered enemy folders and 14 numbered hero folders, each providing sprite animation frames for actions such as Idle, Walk, Run, Jump, Fall, Attack, Bow, Damage, and Dead.

## Tilesets

- PS_Tileset_01.png through PS_Tileset_15.png: outdoor platformer tilesets (grass, dirt, stone, brick, sand, snow, cave, forest, sky, candy, hell, space, ice, dungeon, cloud).
- PS_Tileset_01_2.png and PS_Tileset_01_Full.png: alternative layouts and full preview of the main tileset.
- Animated Tiles folder: animated water, lava, and torch tiles.

## Animated Objects

- PS_Coins_1–4.png: spinning gold coin animations.
- PS_Crystal_Black_1–5.png, Blue_1–5.png, Green_1–5.png, Yellow_1–5.png: pulsing gem sprites.
- PS_Items_Animated_Sheet.png: sheet of collectible item sprites.
- PS_Boxes_Explosive, PS_Boxes, PS_Blocks, PS_Lever: destructible and interactive blocks.
- PS_Flag_1, PS_Goal, PS_Sign: flags and goal markers.
- PS_Bush_1, PS_Plant_1, PS_Mushroom_1: foliage and mushroom sprites.
- PS_Heart_1, PS_Star_1, PS_Key_1: pickups and indicators.

## Backgrounds

- Background_14_000–003.png: parallax background layers.
- PS_Background_13_0–3.png: parallax layer set for theme 13.
- PS_Clouds_1–5.png: parallax cloud layers.
- PS_Forest_01–04.png: forest parallax layers.
- PS_Space_Clouds_1–4.png: space parallax layers.
- PS_Tileset_07_Background00–03.png: parallax layers for theme 7.

## Item Icons

- PS_Items.png: sprite sheet of items, pickups, power-ups, and currency.

## Sound Effects

- MP3, OGG, WAV folders: matching platformer sound effects for jumps, coins, hits, deaths, and music loops.

## Engine Builds

- Platformer World [GameMaker].yymps: GameMaker Studio 2 project.
- Platformer World [Unity].unitypackage: Unity import package.
- Platformer World [Godot]: full Godot project folder with main.tscn, project.godot, and ElvGames subproject.
