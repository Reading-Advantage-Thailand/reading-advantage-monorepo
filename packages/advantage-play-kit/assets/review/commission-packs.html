# Commission Packs.zip - Asset Descriptions

This archive contains three modular pixel-art house tilesets, ready for use in RPG Maker or any 2D engine. Each pack includes the main tileset, four palette variants (a/b/c/d), RPG Maker builds, and license files.

## Modular Pixel Houses Pack 01

- mod_houses_01.png: full modular tileset with wooden walls, doors, windows, roofs, fences, signs, and decorative props in a brown and green palette.
- mod_houses_01_a.png: brown-roof palette variant.
- mod_houses_01_b.png: red-roof palette variant.
- mod_houses_01_c.png: blue-roof palette variant.
- mod_houses_01_d.png: alternate wood and accent palette.
- RPG Maker/ folder: tilesets/mod_houses_01_b.png packaged for MV, MZ, and VX.
- License.txt and Read Me.txt: usage terms.

## Modular Pixel Houses Pack 02

- mod_houses_02.png: taller houses with multi-pitched roofs, dormer windows, doors, wall segments, and ground tiles in four colour palettes arranged in one sheet.
- mod_houses_02_a.png, _b.png, _c.png, _d.png: individual palette sheets matching the main palette sections.
- RPG Maker/ folder: tilesets/mod_houses_02_b.png packaged for MV, MZ, and VX.
- License.txt and Read Me.txt: usage terms.

## Pixel Farming Houses Pack

- com_houses_05.png: full barn-style house tileset with wooden walls, sliding doors, and large barn silhouettes.
- com_houses_05_a.png, _b.png, _c.png, _d.png: palette variants in blue-grey, dark grey, green-roof, and red-roof tones.
- RPG Maker/ folder: tilesets/com_houses_05_b.png packaged for MV, MZ, and VX.
- License.txt and Read Me.txt: usage terms.

## Notes

This archive contains building tilesets only. There are no characters, monsters, or creatures in this pack.
