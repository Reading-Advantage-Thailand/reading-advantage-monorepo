# Pixel VFX.zip - Asset Descriptions

This archive contains pixel-art visual-effect sprite animations for spells, buffs, debuffs, hits, and stat changes. Each effect ships in 1x, 2x, and 3x scaled versions plus RPG Maker-ready builds.

## Buff and Debuff Pixel VFX

Stat icons that appear over characters. Each PNG contains a vertical animation strip.

- Accurracy_Down.png, Accurracy_Up.png: accuracy arrows.
- Aim_Down.png, Aim_Up.png: aim crosshair indicators.
- Attack_Down.png, Attack_Up.png: sword arrows for attack stat.
- Block_Down.png, Block_Up.png: shield arrows for block stat.
- Charm_Down.png, Charm_Up.png: heart arrows for charm stat.
- Defense_Down.png, Defense_Up.png: armor arrows for defence stat.
- Gold_Down.png, Gold_Up.png: coin arrows for gold-find stat.
- Haste.png, Haste_Glow.png: green speed-up ring that surrounds a character.
- Health_Down.png, Health_Up.png: heart arrows for health stat.
- Hit01.png, Hit02.png, Hit03.png: red impact bursts.
- Loot_Down.png, Loot_Up.png: chest arrows for loot stat.
- Luck_Down.png, Luck_Up.png: clover arrows for luck stat.
- Magic_Down.png, Magic_Up.png: wand arrows for magic stat.
- Message.png: blank notification frame.
- Protect_Down.png, Protect_Up.png: shield arrows for protection stat.
- Resistance_Down.png, Resistance_Up.png: arrow icons for resistance stat.
- Slow.png, Slow_Glow.png: blue slow-down ring around a character.
- Speed_Down.png, Speed_Up.png: boot arrows for speed stat.

The same files are duplicated in the Buffs & Debuffs Scaled 2x and Buffs & Debuffs Scaled 3x folders at 2x and 3x resolution. The RPG Maker folder contains pre-built MV, MZ, and VX animations.

## Fire Spells Pixel VFX

- Charge_Fire.png: charging-up fire animation.
- Fire_Explosion.png: large fire blast explosion.
- Fireball.png, Fireball_Small.png: fireball projectile sprites.
- Fireball_Hit.png, Fireball_Small_Hit.png: fireball impact sprites.
- Flame_Explosion.png: short-range flame burst.
- Lavaball.png, Lavaball_Hit.png: lava-coloured fireball sprites.
- Spawn_Fireball.png: fireball spawn-in animation.

Scaled 2x and Scaled 3x folders plus RPG Maker folder are included.

## Ice Spells Pixel VFX

- Arctic_Blast.png: wide-area ice blast.
- Frost_Trap.png: floor-frost trap placement.
- Frostball.png, Frostball_Cast.png, Frostball_Hit.png: iceball projectile.
- Frostbite.png, Frostbite_Break.png: frostbite debuff icon and shatter effect.
- Frozen_Crystal.png: static frozen crystal.
- Ice_Prison.png, Ice_Prison_Break.png: ice prison cage and shatter.
- Ice_Shard.png, Ice_Shard_Cast.png, Ice_Shard_Hit.png: ice shard projectile.

Scaled 2x and Scaled 3x folders plus RPG Maker folder are included.

## Light Spells Pixel VFX

- Lightning_Ball.png, Lightning_Ball_Y.png: lightning ball projectiles in blue and yellow variants.
- Lightning_Bolt01.png, Lightning_Bolt01_Y.png, Lightning_Bolt02.png, Lightning_Bolt02_Y.png: vertical lightning bolts.
- Lightning_Cast.png, Lightning_Cast_Y.png: cast-from-hand sparks.
- Lightning_Explosion.png, Lightning_Explosion_Y.png: impact explosion.
- Lightning_Hit.png, Lightning_Hit_Y.png: hit impact.
- Lightning_Trap.png, Lightning_Trap_Y.png: floor lightning trap.
- Thunder01.png, Thunder01_Y.png, Thunder02.png, Thunder02_Y.png: thunder strike.

Scaled 2x and Scaled 3x folders plus RPG Maker folder are included.

## Notes

This archive contains visual-effect animations only. There are no character, monster, or creature sprites in this pack.
