# Hand cut lines

Boxes are drawn by looking at the sheet, in the same named-box style as Commission Packs.

Do not crop animation sheets. Do not recut Commission Packs or EvoMonsters. Those already have processed cuts.

Open the overlay PNGs next to the source sheet.

Use `assets/review/cut-editor.html` to zoom, pan, draw, name, and delete boxes. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/packages/advantage-play-kit/assets/review/cut-editor.html?sheet=td-02-a` while the repo is served. Save writes the opened cutlist, or Download JSON.

Sewers tiles are finalized under `processed/sewers-tileset/`. Water-pool frames have the corner frame numbers punched out.
