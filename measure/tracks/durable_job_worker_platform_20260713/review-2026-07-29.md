### 3. Code quality observations

**Good:** the harness's URL guard is genuinely defense-in-depth and worth keeping as-is. Identifier generation is validated before interpolation (`assertGeneratedIdentifier` at `:194-203`) so `adminSql.unsafe` is safe. Cleanup is idempotent via a memoized `cleanupPromise`, aggregates errors rather than swallowing them, and refuses to auto-drop stale scratch databases. Hook ordering is explicit and documented. JSDoc coverage on exported surfaces is complete.

**Weak:**
- The fixture file's contract is `overrides` "layered over a canonical row for the starting state" (`:18`), but no canonical row exists anywhere in the module. The fixtures are unusable as written by Task 11/14 without first building that layer — the abstraction is half-declared.
- `partialTuples(columns, populatedValue)` is column-agnostic by design; the invariant it needs is column-aware. This is the root cause of DWP-T6-H1 and isn't fixable by changing the `"present"` literal.
- The Red suite conflates two concerns — cheap source-text gates and semantic contracts — under names that promise the latter ("declares database-owned append-only replay and control audit privileges and triggers" is a regex over SQL text).
- Coverage on the harness was self-reported at 69.86% branches / 73.68% functions, below the project target, and concentrated exactly in the untested lifecycle branches.

5. **Leaked scratch databases from SIGTERM.** Between `CREATE DATABASE` (`:415`) and handler registration (`:451`) the process default applies; one leak permanently reds the harness until manually cleaned.

No risk to production: nothing shipped touches runtime code.
