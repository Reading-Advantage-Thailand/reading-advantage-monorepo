# Specification: APK Puzzle and Crafting Evidence Cohort

## Scope

Apply the accepted pilot method to Enchanted Library, Rune Match, Alchemist's Synthesis, Potion Rush, Rune Forge Chamber, and Astral Mage.

The track inherits `measure/apk-evidence-cohort-protocol.md`.

## Focus requirements

- Recover exact board, station, recipe, matching, selection, sequencing, conveyor, radial, target, timer, combo, and deterministic shuffle/state behavior.
- Trace prompts, answer choices, duplicate identity, feedback, progression, attempts, scoring, and terminal results.
- Derive short/worst-case Thai and English fixtures from real content boundaries.
- Measure station/node reflow, simultaneous visibility, pointer/touch/typing interactions, HUD, and text fit.
- Keep Astral Mage missing/in-development behavior explicitly provisional and source-classified.

## Acceptance criteria

- Six complete packages cover every assigned denominator item.
- Every puzzle/crafting state and transition has exact evidence.
- No generic workstation or responsive template replaces game-specific behavior.
- Runnable games have real browser evidence with content and input variation.
- Missing claims remain unknown rather than being filled through family analogy.
- No ontology decision is made.
- Zero unresolved Critical, High, or Medium findings.

## Out of scope

- Pilot/other cohorts and cross-game synthesis.
