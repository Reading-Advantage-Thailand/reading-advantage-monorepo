# Specification: Storytime Advantage Program

**Track ID:** `storytime_advantage_program_20261021`
**Type:** feature (classic FR list)
**Status:** proposed. Daniel fixed the program shape and approved the Chibi Sprouts theme on 2026-10-21. Decisions S1 to S14 in [`decisions.md`](./decisions.md) are open. No code and no public date.
**Design:** [`design.md`](./design.md) holds the full program design (exit profile, 128-week sequence, scripts, review deck, graph, content, assessment, kit, home layer, app, MVP path, risks). This spec states the requirements. The design gives the detail.
**Pattern:** the second-subject pattern in `../science_advantage_relaunch_20261006/spec.md` (lesson shape, graph, content, program dimension, engagement, deployment). Storytime is an English program, so its graph extends the English domain and its lesson shape is new.

## Overview

Build Storytime Advantage as the English program for Anuban 2 to P2 (ages 4 to 8) in Thai private schools, with a reading and phonics emphasis. The product is a printed student book and teacher book per level, a card kit, a weekly read-along book and a weekly song with video, a decodable book per phonics element, scripted lessons for five periods a week, and a digital twin (Chibi Sprouts) inside the shared platform. Every item is tagged to a phonics graph under Mastery Advantage, and spaced review is printed into the daily lesson. The hand-off is to Primary Advantage at P3. The first sellable product (MVP) is the print program for Levels 1 and 2 with songs and books on a QR page. The app follows as a second track.

The program serves the Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 Foreign Languages indicators for grades 1 to 3 (F1.1 Gr1/2 "Specify the alphabet and sounds; accurately pronounce and spell simple words") and the GSE Young Learner objectives at GSE 10 to 21, including the nine Primary marks out of scope.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1 Exit profile and backward plan
- The end-of-P2 exit profile is the entry state of Primary Origins 1: the decoding scope by pattern, the 92 sight words, the 13 oral frames, 300 Starters words by ear, writing and speaking outcomes, and the 107 GSE 10 to 21 nodes plus the four sound nodes above 21 (design §1.1).
- Each level has its own exit row (design §1.2). Benchmark 4 of each level measures it.
- Storytime owns a "first words" list. No such list exists today; the union in design §1.1 is the first draft, checked against Origins 1 lesson 1.

### FR-2 Scope and sequence
- The 128-week table in design §2 is the sequence of record: Level 1 letters and the 32-lesson phonemic awareness sequence; Level 2 Reading A-Z elements 1 to 28; Level 3 elements 29 to 51 plus five Thai-specific weeks; Level 4 elements 52 to 68 plus eleven exit-profile weeks. 28 Starters topics cycle once per level.
- The table is generated by [`gen_sequence.py`](./gen_sequence.py) and lives as data in the Workbooks content plan for Storytime. A change to the order is a change to the generator, not a hand edit.
- Each week names: element, phonemic awareness skill, tricky words, sentence frame, vocabulary family, song, read-along, decodable, and the check.

### FR-3 Phonics graph and package
- `mastery-advantage/english/phonics/phonics-knowledge-space.json`, prefix `english.phonics.*`, built and owned by this track; the Graph session reviews (S3). Node kinds and id patterns in design §5.1; the 12-module, 41-skill phonemic awareness taxonomy in §5.2, including a Thai-contrast module; GPC, letter, phoneme, tricky-word, text, and standard nodes in §5.3; about 460 nodes.
- Edges per SPECIFICATION.md §3.7: `aligned_to_standard` only to the `english.phonics.standard.storytime-v1` node, whose sources are the Thai F1.1 indicators and the DfE criteria; `supports` and `equivalent_to` to GSE Young Learner skill nodes; explicit `prerequisite_for` for logical prerequisites; `contains` from text nodes.
- The shared validation gate passes, plus the Storytime rules in design §5.5.
- A lexicon of about 2,000 words with grapheme parses, and two pure functions: `wordWall()` and `decodability()` (design §5.6), with tests. The decodable gate is ratio ≥ 0.90 and zero untaught words, names excepted.
- `packages/phonics-knowledge` from the `science-knowledge` pattern (domain adapter, objective key with `P`, `S`, `W` short ids), and the level map `storytime/storytime-levels.csv` (design §5.7).

### FR-4 Lesson scripts
- Five scripts A to E for Levels 2 to 4 at 49 minutes, the 30-minute compression for Level 2, and five Level 1 scripts at 30 minutes (design §3). Every period opens with the same routine: greeting, calendar, song, review deck.
- Every step uses the four headings of the Primary teacher manual generator (what the teacher does; the English the teacher says; what the students do; what to watch) and ends with Easier, Harder, and Group lines. The teacher reads; the teacher never chooses an activity.
- The teacher book is a build of the Primary manual generator (`Workbooks/dashboard/lib/teacher-manual/`) with a Storytime template. Daniel adds the Thai.

### FR-5 Review deck
- The expanding schedule 2d, 3d, 1w, 2w, 4w, 8w, plus the end-of-level review (design §4). The deck for each day is generated from the sequence and printed as a dated strip, capped at 18 cards and 4 minutes.
- Weak items from the Friday check, reading records, and benchmarks return to the day-2 schedule. The record sheet tells the teacher exactly which items. In the app, the class hot list prints as one line on the next week's strips.

### FR-6 Content production
- All content is made in the Workbooks repo under the Primary production rules: Claude writes every asset, Daniel checks the Thai, the injector writes the database, the app has no generator.
- Decodable books per design §6.1 (84; 8 story pages; decodability gate; cumulative rule; the "Sounds in this book" page; the Pip world drawn young, names decodable at the week). Read-along books per §6.2 (112; the same library re-cut as the leveled class sets aa to C; no separate leveled series for MVP). Songs per §6.3 (112; fixed verse-chorus structure carrying the frame; 24 named gestures; a karaoke cut).
- Phoneme audio: a test of mmx speech on the 44 sounds in month 2; a recorded human phoneme bank if fewer than 40 pass (design §6.4, S5).
- The Workbooks lesson package gains `meta.program = "storytime"` and `meta.shape = "storytime-week"`; a package is one week; the injector key is `(program, level, week)` (design §6.6).

### FR-7 Assessment and placement
- The eight checks in design §7 with their timing, format, and pass marks: letter-sound check, phonemic awareness benchmark (three a year), nonsense-word check, high-frequency word check, reading record (four children a week), scripted speaking assessment (weeks 16 and 32), Friday check, and the placement probe on the Fast Track pattern.
- The Primary catch-up module (design §7.8): a 16-week compressed module hosted in Primary Advantage with `program = primary` and a `phonics-catch-up` module tag, Storytime scripts A and C, cards, and decodables; no new books. It is a post-MVP deliverable of this track with Primary as the host app.

### FR-8 Teacher kit and training
- The printed kit per level, the student book, the classroom language card, and the 14 routine videos in design §8.
- The 2-day training and the fidelity protocol are reused with a Storytime day 2 and a Storytime checklist. No new training format.

### FR-9 Home layer
- MVP: the weekly Thai home card perforated from the student book, the song and read-along on a public QR page (no login), and a one-page Thai parent report at weeks 16 and 32 generated by the teacher. Later: the at-home app view by QR card login and the caregiver tips library (design §9).

### FR-10 Digital twin: Chibi Sprouts (post-MVP track within this track)
- World, loop, and interface rules in design §10.1 and §10.2: the home village of the Chibi Quest world; the cast at age 5; stars and seeds; the pet; the picture map; the class garden; no combat and no defeat state; sound-first; QR login; tap targets ≥ 64 px; no typing.
- App MVP features in §10.3: book shelf in the Primary read-along player, song player with karaoke, sound cards with the phoneme bank, three item types, three cartridges, the teacher views, FSRS per child.
- Three cartridges with a new `grapheme` input mode (§10.4). Evidence policy `storytime-evidence.v1` on the Primary row shape (§10.5) and the durable job `storytime.mastery.evidence`.
- Platform contracts (§10.6): `programSchema` gains `storytime` (requested from the Science track now, S8); `objectiveSkillSchema`, `shortIdSchema`, `evidence-policy.ts`, and `record-evidence.ts` gain Phonics, Speaking, and Writing (one task); hosting in the shared container under a storytime hostname with amber brand tokens.

### FR-11 MVP and milestones
- MVP = Option B in design §11: Levels 1 and 2 print-first, songs and books by QR, benchmarks, training, no app. Option A (Level 2 only) is the stop-rule fallback. Milestones are relative (months 1 to 8) until Daniel sets dates. Capacity and Daniel's hours in §11.3; stop rules in §11.4.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- No user-facing text names a launch date. Customer copy uses CEFR only. The hand-off sentence (S12) is the same in every PR file.
- Reading A-Z, Jolly Phonics, and Developing Sound Sense are references. No text, word list, song, game, or picture is copied. The graph cites frameworks (GSE, DfE criteria, the Thai curriculum), not vendors.
- Every asset comes from advantage-forge or the Workbooks pipelines. No licensed pixel assets. Prompts are race-unmarked.
- Backend code follows AGENTS.md: contracts in Zod, business logic in domain modules, tenant scoping through `createTenantDB`, tests with every change, no new cryptographic hashing.
- No change to shared packages before the owner's go for the app track. The print MVP needs no platform change except the QR page.
- Every claim in this program is deliverable by the Workbooks pipeline, Forge, and the shared platform as they exist on 2026-10-21.

## Non-Goals

- Level 3 and Level 4 content, and the catch-up module, in the MVP.
- A parent login, speech recognition, handwriting recognition, or choreography scoring.
- Reedy. A separate leveled reader series. A separate Storytime mobile app.
- Any change to the Primary Origins books.

## Acceptance Criteria (MVP)

1. The phonics graph passes the shared validation gate and the Storytime rules; the Graph session has reviewed it.
2. `wordWall()` and `decodability()` pass their tests, and every Level 2 decodable passes the 0.90 gate with zero untaught words.
3. Level 2 week 1 exists as one Workbooks package with the five scripts, the decodable, the read-along, the song with video and karaoke cut, the review strips, the check sheet, and the home card; Daniel's Thai check is recorded.
4. All 28 Level 2 weeks and all 28 Level 1 weeks are in the canonical store with node ids on every row.
5. The teacher book for each level builds from the manual generator with the four headings and the three variant lines on every step.
6. The song and read-along of any week open from the home card QR in the read-along player without a login.
7. The four benchmark packs per level exist with scripts, forms, and pass marks.
8. The print proof of the Level 1 and Level 2 student and teacher books and the card kits is approved by Daniel.
9. The training day 2 and the 14 routine videos exist in Thai.
10. One school runs Anuban 2 and Anuban 3 for eight weeks with weekly fidelity visits, and the week-8 benchmarks are recorded.
