Primary Advantage RPG skin: Phase 1 for review

6 October 2026. The scene system, the student shell, and the home page in the guild hall are app code now. The captures below come from the running app with the QA student, at 375 px and 1280 px, in day and night mode.

What changed. Every student page now sits in the Chibi Quest world: a wooden header with the coin purse and the XP gem, a signpost of planks on desktop, a wooden toolbar with Forge icons on phones, and a fixed Forge backdrop behind the page. The home page is the first page on the new look.

The 2D and 3D switch. The switch is new. One setting in the play kit (@reading-advantage/advantage-play-kit/responsive, localStorage key chibi-quest) serves the ported games and the pages. A device without WebGL2, or a page opened with ?renderer=phaser, gets the 2D view. Phase 1 has no 3D view yet; the hero on the home page is the 2D portrait.

Assets. Every picture comes from advantage-forge. The Forge session owns the rebuild of apps/primary-advantage/public/rpg/ and syncs it after this commit. No ElvGames asset is on any student page.

The parts

PartFileWhat it does
Scenecomponents/rpg/scene.tsxPuts a page in one of the 13 places. The backdrop is a fixed WebP, phone or desktop by width, dimmed at night.
Chromecomponents/rpg/chrome.tsx, styles/rpg.cssPanel, Sign, Banner, Meter, Hearts, Coins, Gem, Plaque, Bubble, and the wood, gold, and iron buttons.
Spritecomponents/rpg/sprite.tsxPlays a Forge sprite strip with CSS steps.
Shellcomponents/rpg/toolbar.tsx, hud.tsx, components/shared/app-layout.tsxThe header HUD, the signpost, and the toolbar for the student area. Staff areas keep the old shell.
Places and artlib/rpg/places.tsThe place list, the backdrop paths, and the icon paths under /rpg/.
Rendererlib/rpg/renderer.ts, use-renderer.tsRe-exports the shared 2D/3D selector from the play kit and gives pages a hook.
Fontsapp/[locale]/layout.tsxFredoka and Mitr from public/rpg/fonts/. Mitr stays until the owner picks Mitr or Mali for Thai.
