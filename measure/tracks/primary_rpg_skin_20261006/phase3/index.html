Primary Advantage RPG skin: Phase 3 for review

6 October 2026. The Class Quest battle and the projector dashboard are on the new look. The captures come from the running app with a staged quest for the QA class, at 375 px and 1280 px, in day and night mode.

What changed. The battle page is the boss arena: the boss idles on the dais as a Forge sprite, its damage meter sits in the wood-and-iron frame with the pending segment, the hero stands on parchment with five hearts and the HP bar, the relics glow when armed, and the game runs inside a stone archway. A hit shakes the boss with a slash, a burst, and a floating number. The result is a sign: the fall plays the death clip and coins rain; a hold shows the boss standing.

The projector dashboard is the battle field: the boss east, every hero west with a name and an HP bar, the big meter on top with the clock and the state sign, the hit feed on parchment, and one gold button for the teacher. A new hit makes the hero lunge and the boss play its hit clip. The field is the 2D sprite field; the 3D field follows the games port and reads the same renderer switch.

What the captures cannot show yet. The committed damage comes from verified game completions, so a staged quest with heartbeats shows the pending segment and a held boss. The fall with the coin rain needs a real run; it is on the Phase 5 list with the promo shots. The game's own briefing card inside the archway keeps its dark look until the Forge session restyles it (spec row 12).
