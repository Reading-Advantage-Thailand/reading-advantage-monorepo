Primary Advantage RPG skin: Phase 0 for review

6 October 2026. The kit, the 13 places at phone and desktop aspect, and three static mocks of the look: the home, the armory, and the battle. Nothing here is app code yet. Approve the look, or send it back with notes, before Phase 1.

Sources. Every picture comes from advantage-forge: scenes re-shot from the 94 maps, item views, boss and NPC renders, sprite strips. The chrome (parchment, planks, banner, meter, hearts, buttons, toolbar) is owned SVG/CSS in kit/chrome/kit.css. No ElvGames asset. The fonts are the Forge's Fredoka and Mitr.

What is real and what is mock. The backdrops, the renders, and the chrome are the real assets Phase 1 will ship. The data on the mocks is test data. The blacksmith idles and the coins fly in the live mock (links below); the effects on the battle mock are the CSS burst, slash, shield flash, shake, and floating number.

What the Forge still needs for Phase 1. Transparent view renders from the pipeline (the studio grey was keyed out here with a script); NPC and boss strips exported per clip at one frame size; the avatar composer's 3D view with a portrait fallback through the games' 2D selector.
