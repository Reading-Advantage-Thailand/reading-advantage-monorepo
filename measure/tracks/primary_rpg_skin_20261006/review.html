Primary Advantage: the RPG skin, page by page

A plan for the owner's review, 6 October 2026. Each card shows the page as it is today, the Forge scene it moves into, three Forge props it uses, and the promo moment it must produce. The spec with the phases and the open questions is spec.md beside this page.

Rules. Every asset comes from advantage-forge; no ElvGames asset appears in the app. Reading stays protected: the story text column gets a frame, not decorations. Backdrops are static renders for old phones; three.js runs only where the avatar must move. One scene system serves every page. The owner sees captures before a page is called done.

The bar. "How am I supposed to create an impactful marketing video from any of this?" A screen that is not worth five seconds in a promo video is not done.

Phases. 0: the Forge UI kit (parchment panel, plank sign, banner, meter frame, hearts, coins, slot icons, three effect sheets), the 12 backdrops, and three static mocks (shop, battle, home) for the owner. 1: the scene system, the shell, the home. 2: picker, inventory, shop. 3: the battle phone page and the projector dashboard. 4: the remaining pages. 5: the five promo shots recorded from the build.

Home /student/home

Plan. Stats on a wooden plaque. Today's lesson is a notice pinned on the board. The class book is the open book on the map table. Class Quest is a banner with the boss portrait and a wood-and-iron meter. Continue reading is a scroll. Games is the arena door.

Promo moment. The hero walks in and the quest banner unrolls with the boss's face.

Avatar picker /student/avatar?from=me

Plan. The 15 heroes stand in a half-circle. Tap one and it steps forward and plays idle. Colours are dye pots and potion bottles on a stone table; the hero changes live. Save: the hero bows.

Promo moment. The chosen hero stepping into the light.

My avatar (inventory) /student/avatar

Plan. A paper-doll: the avatar in the centre, ten slot icons around it. Tap a slot and a drawer opens with the owned pieces as Forge item icons, 8 per page. Tap a piece to wear it. No long scroll.

Promo moment. A helmet landing on the hero.

Avatar shop /student/avatar/shop

Plan. Shelves, not a list. Slot tabs are hanging shop signs. Each shelf shows 6 item icons with coin prices. Locked tiers are a chained chest. Tap an item for a try-on card with the avatar wearing it. On buy, coins fly to the counter and the blacksmith nods. Dyes are paint pots on the try-on card.

Promo moment. Coins flying to the counter and the hat landing on the hero.

Class Quest battle (phone) /student/quest/battle

Plan. Rally: the hero at the campfire by the arena gate, class portraits arriving. Play: the boss on top with its meter in the wood-iron frame, the hero below with five hearts, power-ups as relic icons that glow when armed, the game inside a stone archway. Result: the boss plays its death clip and coins rain, or it roars and holds.

Promo moment. The boss taking a hit and the hearts flashing.

Live dashboard (projector) /teacher/quest/:id/live

Plan. The teaser battle field: the boss east, every student's hero west. A heartbeat makes the hero attack and the boss play hit, with a floating number. HP bars under the heroes, the big meter on top. Rally, battle, result, done as cinematic cuts. The fall plays the death clip and the banner drops.

Promo moment. The boss falling in front of the whole class.

Story list /student/read
