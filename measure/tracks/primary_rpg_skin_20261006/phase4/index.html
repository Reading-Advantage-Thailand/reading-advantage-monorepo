Primary Advantage RPG skin: Phase 4 for review

6 October 2026. Every student page now sits in its place. The captures come from the running app with the QA student, at 375 px and 1280 px; the night frames are in the captures folder.

What changed. Each page returns its scene, puts its heading on the scene, its cards on parchment, its filters and tabs on signs, and its actions on wood, gold, or iron buttons. The client components that own their markup (the game host, the flashcard decks, the lesson rail, the report charts, the assignment list, the story grid) keep their markup; the scene and the panels around them carry the look.

What waits. Lists as objects inside those client components (books on shelves, scrolls in pigeonholes, the lesson marker walking the path, stamped journal pages) are a later pass once the owner accepts this one. The sign-in screens use the gatehouse for every visitor, staff included.
