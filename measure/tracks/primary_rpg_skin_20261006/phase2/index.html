Primary Advantage RPG skin: Phase 2 for review

6 October 2026. The avatar picker, the inventory, and the shop are on the new look. The captures come from the running app with the QA student, at 375 px and 1280 px, in day and night mode.

What changed. The picker is the shrine: the 15 heroes stand on pedestals, the chosen one steps forward in a light, and the colours are dye pots on a parchment table. The inventory is a paper-doll in the treasure vault: the hero in the centre, ten slot icons around it, a drawer of owned pieces for the tapped slot, eight per page. The shop is the armory: the blacksmith greets at the counter, the slot filters are hanging signs, two shelves of six pieces per page, a try-on card with the hero wearing the piece, the dyes as paint pots, and coins that fly to the counter on Buy.

Item pictures. The cards read the Forge view of each piece from /rpg/items/. The Forge build has not shipped those views yet, so the cards show the slot icon of the piece for now. The views appear without a code change once the Forge session syncs its build.

The 3D hero. The hero on these pages is the 2D portrait. The 3D composer arrives with the games port; the pages will then read the shared 2D/3D switch and keep the portrait as the 2D view.
