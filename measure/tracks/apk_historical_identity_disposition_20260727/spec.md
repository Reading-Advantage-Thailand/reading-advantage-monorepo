# Specification: APK Historical/Cancelled Identity Disposition

## Scope

Disposition RPG Battle, The Abyssal Well, Devourer Slime, The Haunted Library, and Babel Architect. These five identities are explicitly gated, not authorized rebuild work.

## Requirements

- Reconcile current, historical, withdrawn, and cancelled status against accepted source evidence and the foundation crosswalk.
- Publish per-identity retain-history, retire-reference, defer, or separately-propose-rebuild decisions with exact sources and owner acceptance.
- Do not introduce a cartridge, placeholder, catalog route, host import, or production-art adoption.
- If a future rebuild is approved, open a new bounded child track rather than changing this disposition scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each identity has one evidence-backed disposition and no implied gameplay or shipping claim.
- All five remain explicitly gated until a separate owner-approved implementation track exists.
