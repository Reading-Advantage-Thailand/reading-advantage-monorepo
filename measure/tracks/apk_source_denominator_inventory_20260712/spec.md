# Specification: APK Independent Source Denominator Inventory

## Overview

Independently discover what must be audited before any mechanic, responsive, capability, or asset interpretation begins. This track depends on accepted `measure_apk_evidence_integrity_gates_20260712` hashes and inherits the replacement program's role and stop-loss contract.

Passing structural tests does not establish factual correctness. The denominator is discovered from raw and historical sources, never from failed or future authored requirements.

## Functional requirements

### FR1: Identity and file denominator

Enumerate catalog identities/aliases, routes, registrations, current and withdrawn implementations, components, logic/config, tests, Reading/Primary copies, assets, audio, data, Measure sources, and exact historical revisions.

### FR2: Scene and state denominator

Enumerate real runtime scenes, modes, overlays, phases, waves, floors, reducers, transitions, terminal states, and presentation surfaces. Synthetic fallback scenes are forbidden.

### FR3: Dual discovery

Use two independent paths: mechanical filesystem/AST/graph discovery and full human source reconciliation by separate subagents. Both record exact discovery method and evidence.

### FR4: Historical and duplicate reconciliation

Distinguish current behavior, historical implementation, active specification, catalog prose, deletion, copy, alias, and unsupported assumption.

### FR5: Asset file denominator

Enumerate every candidate file with canonical path, hash, basic format metadata, identical-hash grouping, roots, and provenance-bearing sidecars. This is discovery, not suitability judgment.

## Required deliverables

- `denominator-discrepancies.json`
- `denominator-method.md`
- Role receipts and full independent review report
- Accepted denominator manifest and hashes

## Acceptance criteria

- Every item has exact file/revision evidence and discovery method.
- Mechanical and human discovery agree or every discrepancy is resolved explicitly.
- Full denominator review is exhaustive, not sampled.
- Candidate asset count matches independent filesystem discovery.
- Duplicate copies are mapped without silent merging or double counting.
- No blueprint, responsive strategy, capability, semantic role, or product disposition is authored.
- Zero unresolved Critical, High, or Medium findings.
- Product owner accepts only the corpus boundary and denominator hashes.

## Out of scope

- Interpreting game intent.
- Normalizing capabilities or assets.
- Browser acceptance beyond discovery of runnable routes.
