# Finance Operations foundation

This track defines the planned `apps/accounting` Finance Operations application.
It is distinct from `apps/accounts` (Company Admin identity), Sales Advantage,
CRM, and statutory bookkeeping.

The track owns provider-neutral operational records and evidence for payees,
expenses, reimbursements, payroll-summary imports, school billing, invoices,
receipts, credit notes, allocations, historical import batches, close periods,
and accountant export packs. A Thai accountant owns accepted books and tax
filings.

Read `spec.md` for the boundary and acceptance contract. Read `plan.md` for
sequenced, dependency-gated execution.
