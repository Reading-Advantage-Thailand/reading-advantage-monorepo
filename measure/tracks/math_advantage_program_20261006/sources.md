# Math graph sources (Phase 0, 2026-10-06)

Read-only research for M3. The graph itself is built in Phase 1 (2026-12-01 to 2027-01-08). Codes are the official ones from the Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551, mathematics learning area, revised B.E. 2560 (three strands: 1 Number and Algebra, 2 Measurement and Geometry, 3 Statistics and Probability; the five mathematical process skills sit outside the strands). Thai text is copied from the official PDF at build time.

## Generator coverage (Phase 2)

The six parametrized generators map to: addition and subtraction (ค 1.1 ป.3/5, ป.3/8, ป.4/8, ป.4/10), multiplication (ป.3/6, ป.4/9), division with remainder (ป.3/7, ป.4/9), fractions (ป.3/4, ป.3/10, ป.4/4, ป.4/13), measurement conversion (ค 2.1 ป.3/5, ป.3/9, ป.4/3), data tables (ค 3.1 ป.3/2, ป.4/1). Word problems (ป.3/9, ป.4/11, ป.4/12) and decimals (ป.4/5, ป.4/6, ป.4/15, ป.4/16) are printed items and a seventh generator if time allows.
