## Semantic safeguards

- Copy strings are atomic or explicitly bounded inventories, never currentness
  or learning-behavior claims.
- Asset hashes prove presence only. No filename, catalog association, or binary
  hash is treated as a call site or scene usage.
- Developer-effort records inventory source surfaces and parsed line counts;
  they intentionally do not estimate person-hours or complexity.
- Each transition claim includes a guard and state mutation/target where the
  source has one. The state union does not prove victory reachability; the exact
  bounded absence query records that no `status = "victory"` assignment was
  found in the historical logic file.
- Four independent negative fixtures refute compound semantic overreach,
  directory citations, fabricated asset usage, and keyword-only responsiveness.
