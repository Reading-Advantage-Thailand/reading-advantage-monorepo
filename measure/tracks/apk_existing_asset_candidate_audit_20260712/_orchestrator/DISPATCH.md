lexicographically sorted effective denominator: the immutable T2 base plus the

game scene-usage slices without changing candidate membership.

## Per-batch dispatch sequence

Use fresh isolated role invocations bound to the same input-freeze digest:

1. `evidence-collector` creates base path records.
2. `mechanical-metadata-inspector` records mechanical metadata only.
3. `caller-analyst` records exact caller/use evidence per path.
4. `provenance-auditor` records exact sidecar/history/license evidence or unknown.
5. `visual-audio-inspector` inspects each group and links every path.
6. `requirements-mapper` joins reviewed facts to accepted T3-T7 usage in slices of
   at most three games and records suitability/disposition or unknown.
7. `truth-test-author` runs batch contracts and counterexample fixtures.
8. `adversarial-reviewer` independently reviews every substantive record and
   disposition with no producer narrative in context.

Specialists supplement rather than replace the five mandatory program roles. No
agent may own more than one producer specialty for a batch, and no producer may test
or review its own or ancestral output.

## Receipt and budget gate

Every applicable role writes a tool-attested receipt at
`role-receipts/<batch-id>/<role>.json` containing the native task name, declared
model, fork mode, allowed input-freeze hash and paths, role boundary, output hashes,
findings, measured resource usage, and final status. Codex does not expose encrypted
raw prompts, hidden ancestry/event IDs, or final-response hashes to repository code;
those fields are documented as unavailable and are never fabricated or reconstructed
through an obsolete OpenCode receipt workflow. Missing available proof, inherited
reviewer context, unmeasured usage, or any ceiling breach fails closed.

The numeric ceilings and stop losses in `phase0-input-freeze-v1.json` are immutable.
Changing one requires prior product-owner approval and invalidates affected outputs.

## Stop immediately when

- any bound input byte or authorization condition drifts;
- one path/group/root/count mismatch appears;
- one unsupported or fabricated factual claim appears;
- a scene-usage slice touches more than three games;
- a receipt is missing, unverifiable, inherited, or over budget;
- two fix/review cycles fail; or
- any Critical, High, or Medium finding remains.

Do not repair predecessor evidence, expand roots, inspect sister repos, infer missing
facts, rerun browsers/gates, or publish success claims. Blockers require an additive
product-owner decision; historical evidence is never rewritten.
