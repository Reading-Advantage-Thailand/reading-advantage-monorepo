## Selection method

Record customer usage where available, owner observations, and direct customer feedback separately.
Compare the decisions students make, rather than game names or art themes.
Estimate rebuild cost after source audits.
Select the smallest portfolio that preserves valued, distinct experiences.
Do not invent a target game count before this comparison.

## Retirement checklist

Record the replacement title and old route behavior.
Preserve historical identifiers, results, and earned rewards.
Keep incomparable scores in their original competition scope.
Update catalogs, tutorials, documentation, and host links together.
Remove unused implementations and assets only after checking remaining consumers.
