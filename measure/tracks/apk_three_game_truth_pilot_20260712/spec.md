# Specification: APK Three-Game Source-Truth Pilot

## Overview

Prove the evidence method cheaply before any corpus-scale work. The default pilot is Dragon Flight, RPG Battle, and The Abyssal Well, representing a large action implementation, a multi-state turn-based implementation, and historical/stale recovery. Final selection must be confirmed from the accepted denominator.

This track depends on accepted evidence-integrity gates and denominator hashes. It inherits all role-separation and stop-loss rules. One unsupported factual claim or denominator mismatch stops the pilot.

## Requirements per pilot game

- Complete claim-evidence ledger with exact source/revision/lines/hashes.
- Reconciled identity, files, copies, routes, tests, scenes, states, transitions, and terminal surfaces.
- Mechanic and learning blueprint derived only from reviewed claims.
- Developer-effort decomposition.
- Current responsive audit and intentional compact/wide contract.
- Real browser evidence for games accepted as runnable under the program definition at required viewports, input modes, Thai/English fixtures, and 1.5x text.
- Concrete scene-level actors, targets, variants, environment, hazards, interactions, VFX, audio, UI, transitions, and results usages.
- Candidate-level asset records for every denominator file.
- Conflicts, unknowns, and bounded confidence.

## Agent separation

Assign at least one distinct evidence collector per game. A separate mapper creates requirements. A truth-test author works from denominator/evidence rather than mapper output. A `fork_turns="none"`, tool-attested adversarial reviewer re-reads every claim against raw source.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every denominator scene/state is represented or explicitly excluded with reviewed evidence.
- Every factual field and atomic proposition resolves to exact evidence; only formatting, stable record IDs, and reviewer notes are exempt.
- Browser evidence exists for every route accepted as runnable under the program definition and covers actual interaction/state preservation.
- Candidate audit row count equals the pilot asset denominator.
- Historical absence is distinguished from incomplete search.
- No generic profile or asset role is accepted without cited equivalence/usage.
- Counterexample injection proves unsupported claims fail.
- Zero unresolved Critical, High, or Medium findings.
- Product owner approves the complete review artifacts—not a summary—before T4–T8 unlock.

## Out of scope

- Any fourth game.
- Cross-game standardization or semantic ontology decisions.
- Successor hashes.
