# Specification: APK Special and Historical Evidence Cohort

## Scope

Apply the accepted pilot method to Griffin Sky-Joust, Realm Carver, Devourer Slime, The Haunted Library, and Babel Architect.

The track inherits `measure/apk-evidence-cohort-protocol.md`.

## Focus requirements

- Recover exact aerial-combat, territory-capture, growth/consumption, multi-floor haunted-library, and sentence-construction behavior.
- Distinguish current implementation, historical implementation, active specification, catalog prose, cancelled renderer work, and unsupported assumption.
- Record exact revisions and claim evidence for deleted or changed sources.
- Reconcile conflicts chronologically without silently preferring a convenient source.
- Preserve bespoke hypotheses without deciding the final capability ontology.

## Acceptance criteria

- Five complete per-game packages cover every assigned denominator item.
- Catalog descriptions never establish detailed mechanics.
- Cancelled designs are labeled design evidence, not implementation fact.
- Missing behavior remains unknown rather than being filled by analogy.
- Every historical claim resolves to a reachable revision and cited-range hash.
- Browser evidence exists for each runnable current implementation.
- Zero unresolved Critical, High, or Medium findings.

## Out of scope

- Pilot and other cohorts.
- Product decisions about whether historical games will ship.
- Ontology synthesis or implementation.
