# Specification: APK Arena & Target Action Wave W4

## Overview

Rebuild the next bounded Advantage Play Kit cohort as shared Phaser 4 cartridges:

- `archers-revenge` — vocabulary aiming and protected-target selection;
- `paladins-twin-soul` — vocabulary arena movement, projectiles, and paired-hero survival;
- `griffin-sky-joust` — sentence-order aerial target action;
- `gryphon-patrol` — sentence patrol, large camera bounds, indicators, and minimap navigation;
- `realm-carver` — sentence-order movement and territory capture.

The cohort follows the accepted W3 generic host, edition, input, persistence, browser, and deletion contracts. Legacy implementations provide mechanic/content evidence only; renderer and transport parity are out of scope.

## Stories

### Story S1: Freeze arena-wave contracts

**As a** game-platform developer
**I want** current browser/source baselines and strict blueprints for all five games
**So that** reusable arena systems preserve educational identity without inheriting legacy defects.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Every game records its actual controls, input mode, target rules, terminal loop, current desktop/mobile defects, and retained content evidence.
- Exact deterministic fixtures, semantic slots, capabilities, control declarations, result mapping, and generic production route are frozen in Red tests.
- An exact cutover skeleton lists every candidate legacy page, component, state/config module, API, test, and asset exception without approving deletion.

### Story S2: Extend reusable arena and target-action systems

**As a** cartridge developer
**I want** deterministic movement, aiming, projectile, wave, aerial-target, indicator, minimap, and territory primitives
**So that** five distinct games share tested infrastructure rather than copied scenes.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Seeded systems deterministically resolve targets, projectiles/collisions, ordered progression, survival, and completion.
- Keyboard, pointer, and touch controls remain equivalent and do not conflict with browser gestures.
- Restart, edition switch, viewport resize, and navigation retain one canvas and one active lifecycle.

### Story S3: Build five dual-edition cartridges

**As a** student
**I want** five visually and mechanically distinct arena/target games
**So that** vocabulary and sentence practice supports aiming, movement, flight, patrol, and territory play.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Each cartridge accepts its declared stable pair-array input and emits exactly one valid five-field `GameResults`.
- Primary Chibi and Secondary Epic use one scene source with edition-owned presentation/tuning.
- Desktop and 390x844 keyboard/pointer/touch runs complete with readable prompts, targets, controls, and results and no horizontal overflow.

### Story S4: Cut over and verify the arena wave

**As a** product owner
**I want** generic-host, persistence, deletion, performance, and browser proof
**So that** public IDs move safely to APK and caller-free legacy code is retired exactly.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All five IDs load through the shared authenticated route and persist server-owned completions.
- Focused new-code coverage exceeds 80%; affected lint/type/test/build, graph, lifecycle, performance, and browser gates pass.
- Mandatory review has no Critical/High finding, and no legacy candidate is deleted before graph/text caller and browser replacement proof.

## Out of Scope

- Defense/combat, collector-adventure, and puzzle-workstation cohorts.
- Multiplayer, leaderboards, new educational ABI fields, client-owned XP, or per-game production hosts.
- Final bespoke asset production; semantic placeholders and edition contracts remain acceptable until dedicated asset work.
