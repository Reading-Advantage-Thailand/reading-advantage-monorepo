## Scope

Revalidate and vertically cut over Archer's Revenge, Paladin's Twin-Soul, Griffin Sky-Joust, Gryphon Patrol, and Realm Carver. These are historical existing-cartridge candidates, not accepted current cutovers.

## Requirements and acceptance

- Follow the same per-title evidence, pinned semantic adoption, selected-output, Advantage Games, Reading, Primary, and exact-retirement sequence as the core cohort.
- Before implementation, freeze every required role's semantic intent, physical
  behavior descriptor, and accepted reuse/ingest/block suitability decision.
- Retain each accepted mechanic and learning loop without treating historical renderer behavior as a requirement.
- Block the individual title rather than substituting it when evidence or host proof is missing.
- Accept only when all five titles have reviewed vertical evidence and no exact legacy callers remain.
