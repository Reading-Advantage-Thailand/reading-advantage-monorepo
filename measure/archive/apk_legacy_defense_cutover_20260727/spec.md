# Specification: APK Legacy Defense Rebuild and Cutover

## Scope

Rebuild and vertically cut over Castle Defense, Wizard vs Zombie, Village Guardian, and Storm the Castle Tower. No placeholder or substitute title is in scope.

## Requirements

- Derive deterministic mechanic and learning tests from accepted evidence before implementation.
- Use approved T11 APIs and separately accepted semantic adoption; a missing capability or mapping blocks that title.
- Consume Asset Contract v2 and accepted suitability/ingestion evidence so asset
  behavior is descriptor-driven rather than inferred from a legacy sheet.
- Prove each rebuilt title through Advantage Games, Reading, Primary, and exact legacy retirement in that order.
- Keep completion domain-owned, validated, tenant-safe, idempotent, and server-authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every scoped title has current compact/wide, real-input, selected-output, two-host, and exact-retirement evidence.
- No cohort-wide or host-wide proof substitutes for per-title evidence.
