# Specification: APK Legacy Puzzle Rebuild and Cutover

## Scope

Rebuild and vertically cut over Enchanted Library, Rune Match, Alchemist's Synthesis, Potion Rush, and Rune Forge Chamber.

## Requirements and acceptance

- Establish Red mechanic and learning-loop tests from accepted evidence for each title.
- Permit only approved T11 interfaces and separately accepted semantic adoption/selected outputs.
- Require Asset Contract v2 and accepted suitability/ingestion evidence before
  implementation. Enchanted Library's walk state must consume its selected
  descriptor's clip behavior, not encode the legacy three-frame count.
- Require individual Advantage Games, Reading, Primary, completion, and exact-retirement proof.
- Keep a blocked title non-playable; do not replace it with a generic puzzle or placeholder.
