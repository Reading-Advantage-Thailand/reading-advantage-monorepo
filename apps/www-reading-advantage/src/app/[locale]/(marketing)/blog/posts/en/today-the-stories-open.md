Storytime Advantage is now live: a full English program for children from Anuban 2 to P2, about ages 4 to 8. The program is open in the app, on the classroom screen and on a phone at home.

## A class day: 30 minutes, every step scripted

Each class day is a 30-minute scripted lesson, and each day brings its own set of activities.

**Letter formation.** Children trace each letter, stroke by stroke, so the hand learns the shape the eye already knows.

**Phonics.** Letter recognition, blending, and segmenting: children hear the sounds inside a word, then put them back together. Every word plays its own clip.

**The oral blend drill.** The teacher models, the class leads, then the teacher tests. Blending gets said out loud, with the whole room.

**Sight words.** A fast step with a timed check, across 100 sight words, so the words that fill every page are ready before the page turns.

**The read-along.** A story read aloud page by page, followed by a fixed set of questions on every read-along. The children know the routine, and the teacher never has to invent one.

**The daily recall.** Five items, every day, so yesterday's words meet the child again today.

Every step is scripted, so a kindergarten or P1 teacher who is not an English specialist can lead the whole day.
