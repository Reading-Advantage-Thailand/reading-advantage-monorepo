# Class Quest: run a battle in 8 minutes

Track `primary_class_quest_20261005`. For teachers and the people who support them.

## Before the week

1. Open the class page or the dashboard and tap **Assign a quest**.
2. Pick a quest, the class, and the battle time. Tap **Assign**.
3. The card shows the boss, the days left, and the class meter. Students see the same card on
   their home page with their goals and power-ups.

The boss target is fixed on the day you assign: 20 damage per student at 70 percent
participation. The battle words come from the class's current lesson glossary, so assign a
book first.

## The battle (8 minutes)

1. Open the class card and tap **Live dashboard**. Put it on the projector.
2. Tell students to open **Go to the battle** on their home page.
3. Tap **Start the rally** (1 minute). Students who open the page appear on the screen.
4. Tap **Start the battle** (5 minutes). Each phone runs the word game. A correct answer is
   2 damage, 3 with a sharp blade. A wrong answer costs 1 HP of 5; a shield blocks one loss.
   The meter moves when a phone saves its game.
5. Tap **Show the result** (2 minutes). The boss fell or held; the helpers are listed in play
   order. Every participant receives 50 GP, plus 25 GP when the boss fell.
6. Tap **Close the quest**.

A refresh of any screen loses nothing. The dashboard polls every 4 seconds; a student's HP
bar dims when the phone has not posted for 60 seconds.

## If something goes wrong

- **The assign page refuses with "no glossary":** assign a class book, or open the next
  lesson that has a glossary, then assign again.
- **A student is missing on the dashboard:** the phone has not opened the battle page, or it
  is signed in as another student.
- **The battle button does nothing:** the quest moved on from another screen. Refresh.
- **Cancel before the battle:** the class card has **Cancel the quest** while the quest is open.
